Gameplay Manual

1. The Objective

Enter a biological war of attrition. You will command either Immune Cells or Diseases. Your goal is to use the unique properties of your cards to deplete the opponent's Health (HP). The first player to win the majority of rounds claims victory.

2. Physical Setup

Before interacting with the terminal, prepare your physical deck:

  • Shuffle: Shuffle the Immune and Disease decks separately.
  • Draw: Draw the first 3 or 5 cards (depending on match length). These must be played in the exact order they were drawn.
  • Study: Players are encouraged to read the biological facts on the back of their cards to strategize.

3. Digital Registration (With Jetson)

If you are using the Jetson camera system, register your cards into the OUTBREAK system first:

  • Coin Flip: The system determines which player is Immune and which is Disease.
  • Scan Pool: Scan your drawn cards one by one until your pool is complete.
  • Wild Selection: Shuffle the Wild cards separately and blindly choose one to scan as your tactical asset.

4. Traditional Gameplay (Without Jetson)

If you are playing a purely physical game without the digital system, follow these protocols:

  • Manual Setup: Use a coin to decide sides and manually track your drawn card order.
  • Deployment: Place your active card down simultaneously with your opponent to avoid reaction bias.
  • Arming Wilds: To activate a Wild card, place it physically on top of your active card.
  • Discarding: After the round resolves, move played cards to a discard pile.

5. The War Room (Mechanics)

Combat is a simulation of cellular warfare based on these metrics:

Base Power: The raw damage dealt per hit.
Health (HP): The total damage a card can absorb before being destroyed.
Multipliers: Biological advantages. Some cards deal 2x damage or take 0.5x damage against specific types.
Attrition: Combat repeats until one card's HP reaches 0.

6. Tactical Arsenal (Wild Cards)

Wilds are tactical strikes that can be "Armed" alongside your active card:

Bacteriophage: Weakens a specific disease card in the opponent's pool, cutting its current HP in half.
Antibiotics: Deals massive damage to all disease cards and minor damage to immune cells across both pools.
Resistance: A defensive shield that halves all incoming damage for the round.
Superbug: A lethal mutation that doubles your damage if you are playing a disease card.
